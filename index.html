No Kings: Vote Early

City Hall

Speakers.

Acacia Park

Performers at the bandshell, community group tables, art projects and mutual aid.

Power belongs to the people, but only if we use it. Make a plan, check your registration, and vote early.

Art project

Two projects in the park:

  1. Draw your protest sign on an index card and add it to a larger canvas. The finished piece will be displayed at the Manitou Art Center.
  2. Bring your "I voted" sticker, or use one we provide, and put it on the VOTE sign to pledge your vote or record that you voted.

This event is nonpartisan and nonviolent. Please do not bring weapons of any kind, including those legally permitted.
