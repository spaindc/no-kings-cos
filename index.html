Mutual aid

Donation drive at Acacia Park. Bring any of these items:

  • Kids' jackets
  • Feminine products
  • Diapers and wipes
  • Toys
  • Hygiene products
  • Nonperishable food

Donations go directly to the community: distributions through the Colorado Springs Mutual Aid & Solidarity Union, the People's Grocery, and monthly free stores.

Art project

Two projects in the park:

  1. Draw your protest sign on an index card and add it to a larger canvas. The finished piece will be displayed at the Manitou Art Center.
  2. Bring your "I voted" sticker, or use one we provide, and put it on the VOTE sign to pledge your vote or record that you voted.
